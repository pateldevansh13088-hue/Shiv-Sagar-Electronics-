Online UPS Systems

True double-conversion power protection delivering regulated pure sine-wave output with zero transfer delay for critical loads.

Typical Applications

Hospital diagnostics, bank servers, ATMs, pathology labs, and IT racks.

Offline / Line-Interactive UPS

Dependable, cost-effective backup protection with automatic voltage boost/buck against sudden grid drops and outages.

Typical Applications

Office desktop workstations, retail POS billing counters, and CCTV systems.

Industrial UPS Systems

Heavy-duty backup power engineered for demanding environments. Direct execution up to 10 kVA; higher kVA coordinated with OEM/project partners.

Typical Applications

PLC automation panels, factory control rooms, and testing laboratories.

Inverters

Commercial and office pure sine-wave power backup systems designed for clean, silent operation of lighting, fans, and computers.

Typical Applications

Offices, clinics, educational institutes, showrooms, and residences.

Voltage Stabilizers

Automatic and servo-controlled voltage regulation protecting sensitive electrical equipment from severe grid surges and under-voltage.

Typical Applications

Medical imaging & lab gear, commercial HVAC, and upstream UPS input protection.

SMF Batteries

Sealed Maintenance-Free (VRLA) batteries offering spill-proof, zero-topping-up energy storage for indoor UPS banks and racks.

Typical Applications

Online UPS battery strings (e.g., 12V 26Ah / 42Ah / 65Ah+), server closets, and offices.

Industrial & Lead Acid Batteries

Tall tubular and heavy-duty flooded lead-acid batteries built for deep-discharge endurance and extended backup autonomy.

Typical Applications

Long-runtime commercial inverter banks, schools, workshops, and substations.

Battery Chargers & Power Supply Systems

Industrial float/boost battery chargers, regulated DC power supplies, isolation transformers, and DIN-rail SMPS units.

Typical Applications

Control panels, DG starter battery banks, telecom racks, and lab benches.
